## Timeline

<div class="timeline-item">
  <div class="tl-date">2016 — Present</div>
  <div class="tl-role">Senior Cloud Infrastructure & Database Platform Engineer</div>
  <div class="tl-company">Sopra Financial Technology</div>

  One of the more complex and rewarding roles of my career. I was brought in to help the organisation move away from on-premises infrastructure and into AWS — and that's exactly what I did.

  **Key responsibilities and achievements:**

  - Designed and built the entire AWS cloud infrastructure from the ground up using **Terraform** as the IaC backbone, ensuring every environment was reproducible and auditable
  - Led the migration of **100+ databases** (Oracle, PostgreSQL, MySQL) from on-premises data centres to AWS — a major multi-year initiative with minimal disruption to live services
  - Used **AWS DMS** to achieve near-zero downtime during database migrations, protecting business continuity throughout
  - Architected and deployed a **Prometheus + Grafana** monitoring stack covering both AWS infrastructure and database-level metrics
  - Set up **AWS Zero ETL integration** between MySQL and Amazon Redshift, giving the analytics team real-time access to production data without complex ETL pipelines
  - Worked closely with application and data teams to recommend the right AWS services and database engines based on their specific workloads and data patterns
  - Authored SOPs, runbooks, and architecture documentation that standardised operational processes across the team
